💡 ADHD Tips & Hacks

⏲️ Time Blindness Combat
Use visual timers, not just alarms. Set 3-4 checkpoints in your day. Let the countdown be your anchor — don't just "feel" when time is up.
⚡ Energy Bursts > Consistency
Stop forcing 8-hour work days. Ride your energy waves. Log 3 intense hours, rest, log again. Track when you're most focused — your "peak hours" are gold.
🎯 Hyperfocus is a Superpower
When you lock in, protect that time. Don't interrupt hyperfocus for "important" stuff. Let the countdown timer run — you'll know when you're done.
😴 Sleep is Your Superpower
One bad sleep day = one bad focus day. Track it. If you're under 6h, expect lower energy. If you hit 8h+, you're golden. Sleep = your most valuable currency.
🧪 Dopamine Hits Matter
Small wins = momentum. Checking off a task, hitting a timer milestone, logging time — these matter. Use them. Your brain needs the hit to keep going.
